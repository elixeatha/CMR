# CMR Primer

An interactive single-page primer on cardiac MRI for cardiologists. Open `index.html` in any modern browser; it has no build step or dependencies (Google Fonts are optional).

Tabs:

1. **Acquisition**: T1/T2 relaxation curves, an in-browser k-space demo (FFT), ECG gating, and the core sequences (bSSFP cine, LGE with an interactive TI/inversion-recovery widget, T1/T2 mapping and ECV, perfusion, phase-contrast with a VENC widget, T2*), with artefacts and fixes, plus a flow and valve section (acquisition and analysis steps, interactive flow–time curves, regurgitation grading, and a phase-image aliasing demo).
2. **Views & protocol**: step-by-step planning of long-axis, short-axis and 2/3/4-chamber views, plus a typical protocol timeline.
3. **Interpretation**: a systematic approach, approximate normal values, schematic short-axis LGE patterns, viability (LGE transmurality widget, dobutamine reserve, trial evidence) and quantitative perfusion (Kellman vs Chiribiri methods compared, MBF/MPR interpreter), mapping values and classic disease appearances.
4. **Research techniques**: myocardial energetics and 31P spectroscopy (interactive spectrum), 1H spectroscopy of myocardial lipid, hyperpolarised 13C, diffusion tensor CMR, and a horizon scan of other emerging methods.
5. **Seminal papers**: a curated reading list with DOI or PubMed links.
6. **Quiz**: 45 multiple-choice questions with topic filter, score tracker and explanations.
7. **About**: credits, disclaimer and a feedback form that opens a pre-filled email to Jennie.han@nhs.net.

Numbers are rounded teaching values. Use local reference ranges and current guidelines for clinical reporting.
